AI Chat

Designing a clear and intuitive AI-powered chat experience focused on simple interaction and usability.

AI Web App UX/UI Responsive Design

My role

UI/UX Designer

Timeline

1 month

Team

Client · Developer · UI/UX Designer

Focus

UX/UI · AI Interface · User Flows · Responsive Design

The challenge

Making AI interaction feel simple and intuitive.

AI chat interfaces can quickly become visually complex as conversations, controls, and supporting actions compete for attention. The challenge was to keep the interaction clear while making the conversation the primary focus.

My approach

Reduce complexity. Keep the conversation central.

I built the interface around a clear conversation flow, using familiar chat patterns, focused navigation, and a restrained visual hierarchy to keep primary actions easy to find without distracting from the conversation.
