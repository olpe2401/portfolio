HuisLeon

Turning complex property data into a clear and intuitive experience.

Data-rich Web Platform UX/UI Complex Interface

My role

UI/UX Designer

Timeline

2 months

Team

CEO · Developer · UI/UX Designer

Focus

UX/UI · Information Architecture · Data Visualization · Responsive Design

The challenge

Making large amounts of property data easier to understand.

HuisLeon combines maps, charts, tables, property listings, and market information in one platform, creating a complex information environment for users. The challenge was to organize this data into a clear interface without overwhelming users.

My approach

Structure the data. Simplify the experience.

I defined a clearer information hierarchy and page logic, then built reusable layouts for maps, property details, tables, and analytics to make complex data easier to scan, compare, and navigate.
