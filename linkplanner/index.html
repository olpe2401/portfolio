LinkPlanner

Redesigning a complex SEO platform to make services, information, and user journeys clearer.

B2B SaaS SEO UX/UI Design System

My role

UI/UX Designer

Timeline

1 month

Team

CEO · Developer · UI/UX Designer

Focus

UX/UI · Information Architecture · Design System · Web Design

The challenge

Making a complex SEO platform easier to navigate.

The platform contained a large number of SEO services, pages, and content, making it difficult for users to quickly understand the offering and find relevant information.

My approach

Structure first. Visuals second.

I restructured the information architecture, clarified page hierarchy, and introduced reusable UI patterns to make complex content easier to scan and keep the experience consistent across the platform.
